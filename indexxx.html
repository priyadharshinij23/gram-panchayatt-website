Where is my application?

Every application shows its status. An official can approve it, reject it, or send it back with a written reason. Nothing is approved automatically.

    How the site is meant to work

    One site, three modules. Residents get simple pages in Tamil and English. Officials see pending work first. The admin looks after accounts, content and the audit trail.

      Quality targets
      500+people using the site at once
      < 4 spage load on a 3G connection
      99.5%uptime, apart from planned maintenance
      AES-256encryption for stored sensitive data, TLS when sent
      Dailydatabase backup
      • Passwords are hashed, and an account locks after too many failed logins.
      • Large readable text, short forms, phone-first layout, and basic accessibility for people with visual or motor difficulties.
      • Built so it can later connect to district and state e-governance portals and serve other panchayats.
      Rules the system follows
      • All traffic between users and the site goes over SSL/TLS.
      • Personal data follows Indian IT and data protection rules, and is collected only when a service needs it.
      • An official checks every application and its documents before approving.
      • Access depends on role, so each person sees and changes only what their job allows.
      • Every approval, payment and content change is saved in the audit log.
      • Notices, budgets and minutes stay public, with no login.

      Technology options
      • Database: MySQL or PostgreSQL
      • Backend: Node.js, Django or Spring Boot
      • Frontend: React, Angular, or plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript
      • Outside services: payment gateway (UPI, cards, net banking), SMS gateway, email service
      Future scope
      • Links with state and national e-governance portals, such as e-Sevai and other Digital India services
      • A mobile app that works offline where internet is weak
      • A Tamil voice assistant or chatbot for people who find forms hard
      • Live streaming and recordings of Gram Sabha meetings
      • Dashboards on village development: water, sanitation, scheme progress
